# PhantomBotAI

## Vision

PhantomBotAI is an AI-native revenue recovery and conversational commerce platform for Shopify.

The platform is designed to:

- detect shopper hesitation
- recover abandoned revenue
- increase conversion rates
- improve AOV
- automate recovery workflows
- transform support conversations into monetization opportunities

---

# Core Thesis

Most Shopify brands lose revenue because:

- shoppers hesitate
- trust breaks
- product uncertainty exists
- checkout friction increases
- support conversations fail to convert

PhantomBotAI is designed to identify these behavioral patterns and execute adaptive interventions before the shopper leaves.
