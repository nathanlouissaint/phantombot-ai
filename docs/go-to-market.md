# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Initial Goal

Acquire first 10 Shopify stores.

---

# Distribution Channels

## Primary

- LinkedIn
- Twitter/X
- Shopify communities
- cold outreach
- founder-led demos

---

# Positioning

"AI Revenue Recovery Agent for Shopify"

NOT:
- chatbot
- support desk
- analytics tool

---

# Core Promise

Recover revenue from high-intent shoppers before they abandon.

---

# Merchant Pain Points

- abandoned carts
- low conversion rate
- support overload
- weak upsells
- trust issues
- poor product discovery

---

# Acquisition Strategy

## Phase 1

Manual onboarding.

Work directly with merchants.

Collect:
- recovery patterns
- support pain points
- hesitation examples
- checkout friction data

---

# Expansion Strategy

## Phase 2

Case studies.

Focus on:
- recovered revenue
- conversion lift
- support reduction

---

# Long-Term Distribution

- Shopify App Store
- strategic partnerships
- ecommerce agencies
- retention consultants
- Shopify growth operators
